# Kjapp Utsendelse av Kolli

## v2.1.0

- **New name and look:** [Soeta's] Kjapp Utsendelse av Kolli, with the [Soeta's] title, font and colors in the settings window, a minimap button and an add-on compartment entry. The command is `/kuk`.
- **To field:** always fills in the plain character name, never "Name-Realm". Alts are found whatever realm name the game reports, and the guild and friends lists are refreshed when you click into the To field. New `/kuk find gi` shows what each source finds.
- **QuickAttach profiles per character**, and Jewelcrafting, Inscription and Optional Reagents hidden by default.
- **Automatic subject** from the first item.
- **Items in the extra slots look greyed out in your bags.**
- Default of **36 slots** (three letters of 12).
